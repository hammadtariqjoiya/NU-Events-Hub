Venue Booking

Societies planning a campus event should first discuss the venue, date and expected attendance with the relevant university administration.

  1. Decide the event date and expected number of participants.
  2. Choose a suitable venue and prepare the event proposal.
  3. Submit the proposal to the relevant university office.
  4. Wait for confirmation before announcing the booking.
Submit an event proposal

Event Rules

  • Respect university policies and campus facilities.
  • Obtain approval before arranging an event.
  • Keep the venue clean and return equipment after use.
  • Make sure activities are safe and welcoming for participants.
  • Share accurate event information with attendees.
